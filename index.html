I am a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Purdue University, advised by Prof. Vijay Raghunathan. My research sits at the intersection of machine learning and computer architecture, with a focus on making AI systems dramatically more energy-efficient for deployment at the edge.

I have worked on enabling complex AI workloads, including graph neural networks, state space models, emerging language-model blocks, and multimodal cognitive systems, to run efficiently on resource-constrained neural processing units (NPUs). My work spans the full stack: from algorithmic approximations to hardware-software co-design, silicon-aware optimization, and the design of heterogeneous NPUs themselves.

During my PhD, I have interned twice at Intel (Client AI Research in 2024 and AI Solutions Engineering in 2026), work that led to four Intel patent families and a public Intel developer webinar. Prior to Purdue, I spent time at Texas Instruments (Design Verification & Validation) and IIT Kharagpur (Research Intern), building a rare breadth across industry and academia.

Research Highlights

Building the bridge between AI algorithms and efficient hardware realization.

2026ACM TECS 2026

NEXUS

GNNs & SSMs on Commercial NPUs

A unified enable–optimize–trade-off methodology for running emerging AI workloads on off-the-shelf NPUs, building on GraNNite and XAMBA.

  • Up to 10.8x GNN speedup vs. CPU
  • Up to 3.8x lower SSM latency
GitHub Paper

2026IEEE IISWC 2026

TESSERA

Design-Space Exploration for Heterogeneous NPUs

Workload-driven simulator and DSE framework that jointly explores tile mix, MAC arrays, precision, dataflow, and special-function units for heterogeneous NPUs. Co-first author.

  • 46.9% average energy savings vs. the best homogeneous NPU of equal area, across 20 workloads
“the first framework to comprehensively target the design of flexible multi-engine DL accelerators”Researchers at Chalmers University of Technology, on TESSERA (first posted as MOSAIC), in MEDEM, arXiv 2026
Paper arXiv

2026ICEdge 2026

Emerging LMs on NPUs

Gated DeltaNet & Liquid Foundation Models on Client NPUs

To our knowledge, the first characterization of these attention-replacing language-model blocks on a client NPU. Arithmetic intensity, not FLOP count, predicts NPU cost. Co-first author.

  • 170x fewer MACs per token, yet only 4–6x faster (LFM vs. GDN)
  • Bounding the state halves decode energy
To appear

2026IEEE TCASAI 2026

DYNNER

Dynamic Power Reduction in DL Accelerators

Compiler-level weight reordering that minimizes bit-level switching in processing elements: the first approach to cut switching-induced active power with no hardware changes and no accuracy loss.

  • Up to 50% less layer-level switching
  • 6% lower total active power
Paper Patent

2026ISLPED 2026

COSMOS

Energy-Efficient Multimodal Cognitive Systems

Closed-loop runtime framework that uses semantic context and temporal stability to govern modality activation, inference rate, and approximation on multimodal edge nodes.

  • 7.73x longer lifetime
  • <5% accuracy loss

Best Lightning Talk Award, 2026

Paper

2025IJCNN 2025

GraNNite

Graph Neural Networks on NPUs

End-to-end deployment flow that enables, optimizes, and approximates GNN execution on resource-constrained NPUs with hardware-aware operator mappings. Intel featured it in a public developer webinar.

  • 2.6–7.6x faster than default NPU mappings
  • Up to 8.6x energy gain vs. CPU/GPU
GitHub Paper Webinar

2025ICLR Workshop 2025

XAMBA

State Space Models on NPUs

Enables efficient SSM execution on NPUs by reformulating sequential cumulative sums, reductions, and activations into matrix-based, hardware-friendly primitives (CumBA, ReduBA, ActiBA).

  • Up to 4.8x speedup on an Intel Core Ultra Series 2 AI PC
GitHub Paper

2025ISLPED 2025

ECO

Context-Aware Multimodal AI on NPUs

Runtime-tunable, NPU-aware knobs (approximate interpolation, quantization, and model scaling) that adapt RGB and LiDAR segmentation to energy availability and sensor reliability on an Intel Core Ultra NPU.

  • Up to 4.9x performance vs. CPU
  • 11.3x energy efficiency vs. CPU

Best Poster, SRC CoCoSys 2025

GitHub Paper Video

2025COINS 2025

PRISM

Processing-In-Sensor+Memory for Battery-Free IoT

Energy-efficient AIoT architecture combining processing-in-sensor and processing-in-memory to run binary neural networks on intermittently powered, battery-free devices.

  • 110.7x forward progress vs. MCU-only
Paper

2024IEEE IoTJ 2024

DRAX

Collaborative Inference with Multi-System Approximations

Energy-efficient collaborative inference framework enabling coordinated approximation across distributed edge devices. Its distributed approximate systems idea is discussed in a 2025 ACM Computing Surveys survey.

  • 2.6–8x energy savings
  • <1% quality loss
GitHub Paper

2020–2023IEEE TC 2023

HIPER & HIPEDAP

Hardware for Hidden-Periodicity Detection

Accelerators for Ramanujan filter banks, which find hidden periodic patterns in signals such as DNA and EEG, from a multiplier-less design to approximate microarchitectures. Undergraduate work.

  • 4.7x smaller, 8.2x faster clock, 1.7x lower power (45 nm)
  • Up to 32.2% more power savings with approximation
